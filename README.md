# simester-prism-guide
How to Get the Good Ending

Chapter 1 — First Encounter

Choose: "You seem... lonely"

Choose: "Tell me about the valley. What happened here?"

Choose: "Have you been here long? Why did you stay?"

Choose: "I can see you're hiding something. What is it?"

Choose: "You know more about me than you say, don't you?"

Choose: "Alright, but I want to understand who you really are"

Chapter 2 — Growing Attraction

Choose: "Something feels wrong. Is this real?"

Choose: "But what do you really feel?"

Choose: "These words are beautiful, but they’re hiding pain, aren’t they?"

Choose: "You disappeared. What’s happening? Why do you do that?"

Choose: "I want to be with you, but I need to understand what's going on"

Choose: "Yes, but answer my questions first"

Choose: "I feel like something is wrong with this place"

Chapter 3 — Uncovering the Truth

Choose: "Are these the ones you lost?"

Choose: "Are they trapped here? Tell me the truth"

Choose: "This must hurt so much"

Choose: "I understand your pain, but it doesn’t justify what you’re doing"

Choose: "Tell me about her. The real story"

Choose: "What happened to her? How did you lose her?"

Choose: "I don’t want to be part of your collection. I want to help you"

Chapter 4 — The Final Threshold

Choose: "I'm scared, but I'm still listening. Don't shut me out"

Choose: "You're afraid you'll destroy everything again? Tell me the truth"

Choose: "I accept you as you are. But you have to let me stay by your side"

Choose: "I’ll stay, but only if you're ready to fight too — not hide"
